The Problem

  • Medicine Shortages: PHCs stockpile or stockout with no visibility
  • Bed Crises: One PHC overwhelmed, another empty. No coordination.
  • Decision Delays: Paper-based approvals. 48+ hours for supply transfers.
  • Patient Loss: 15-20% of emergency cases turned away due to "no beds"
  • Data Silos: No state-wide view of demand, supply, or capacity
Impact: Preventable deaths. Wasted resources. Eroded public trust.
